# CHARACTER PROFILE: Kora Chen-Vega
## Street Survivor → Consciousness Guide

### FULL PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

**Age**: 26 years old
**Ethnicity**: African descent (mixed heritage)
**Build**: 5'6" (168 cm), athletic and muscular from physical resistance work, street-hardened physique
**Hair**: Natural texture, varies in length (freedom from regulation), often worn in practical styles that allow quick movement
**Eyes**: Dark brown with indigo flecks that become pronounced during Cascade sensitivity episodes
**Distinctive Features**: 
- Link extraction scar behind right ear - raised ridge of puckered tissue that throbs with phantom neural activity
- Defensive wounds on forearms - precise cuts from deflecting edged weapons rather than accidental injuries
- Silver-pink jagged scar along jawline from recent Cascade episode damage
- Indigo bioluminescence visible beneath skin during consciousness episodes, creating circuit-like patterns
- One chipped canine tooth, rough edge where tongue occasionally touches

**Attire Evolution**:
- **Practical Resistance Gear**: Worn leather jacket, reinforced boots, utility clothing built for survival
- **Color Palette**: Earth tones (brown, olive, dark gray) contrasting with optimization sterility
- **Technology Integration**: Primitive communication devices, manual switches and buttons, bone-conduction implants
- **Cascade Episodes**: Clothing that accommodates sudden consciousness expansions and physical stress responses

**Mannerisms**:
- Unconscious thirteen-step pacing pattern when stressed (trauma response from childhood)
- Defensive stance with weight balanced for quick movement
- Fingers tap counting rhythms against thigh during danger assessment
- Street-hardened alertness - constantly scanning for threats and escape routes
- Language switches between casual Slums dialect and tactical precision depending on situation
